## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a warm, soothing, and radically accessible sensory sanctuary designed specifically for special education elementary students. Built around the concept of gentle self-regulation and emotional scaffolding, the interface channels a calm picture-book aesthetic. The goal is to diffuse anxiety, prevent sensory overload, and empower neurodiverse children to identify, articulate, and soothe their feelings without shame or confusion.

The style unites soft minimalism with tactile, rounded storybook forms. Instead of jarring alarms, chaotic gamification, or visually dense dashboards, the aesthetic is deliberately spacious, organic, and predictable. Tactile affordances mimic friendly stickers and padded wooden play blocks, while a cheerful, steadfast cat companion acts as a consistent grounding guide across every interaction. Visual cues prioritize clarity, non-verbal comprehension, and generous touch feedback.

## Colors

The palette is engineered to calm overstimulated nervous systems while providing unmistakable, high-contrast feedback for children with cognitive and visual differences.

- **Primary (`#4EBA87` - Soothing Mint Green):** Represents safety, calm breathing, and successful grounding. Paired with tint `#E8F7F0` for relaxed container fills.
- **Secondary (`#5B9BD5` - Gentle Sky Blue):** Represents clarity, pacing, and reflective mindfulness. Accompanied by `#EBF4FC` for tranquil state changes.
- **Tertiary (`#A288E3` - Soft Lavender):** Represents quiet restoration and deep imagination. Paired with `#F2EDFC` for supportive introspective actions.
- **Supportive Peach / Warm Apricot (`#FFA86B`, `#FFF0E5`):** Denotes energetic engagement, cheerfulness, and positive encouragement without the urgency of harsh orange.
- **Canvas Neutrals (`#FDFBF7` canvas, `#F7F4EB` surface card):** Warm ivory and oatmeal tones prevent the harsh glare and visual fatigue associated with pure white (`#FFFFFF`). Text utilizes a warm, deep charcoal-brown (`#36312C`) for softer optical reading contrast that maintains WCAG AAA accessibility.
- **Emotion Regulation Spectrum:**
  - *Calm & Comfortable:* `#4EBA87` (Mint)
  - *Mild Discomfort / Rising Energy:* `#F5A623` (Warm Amber Yellow)
  - *Overwhelmed / Frustrated / Upset:* `#EE6B6B` (Soft Warm Coral Red — never aggressive crimson)

## Typography

Typography prioritizes hyper-legibility, open counters, rounded apexes, and generous vertical rhythm. This accommodates early readers, students with dyslexia, and children experiencing situational cognitive distress.

- **Headlines (`Plus Jakarta Sans` / rounded hangul complement):** Features friendly terminals and sturdy structural curves. It brings an encouraging, storybook warmth without tipping into illegible novelty.
- **Body & Instructional Copy (`Noto Sans`):** Balanced, unpretentious, and exceptionally legible in Korean and multilingual environments. Generous line heights (1.5–1.7x) ensure tracking remains effortless across reading levels.
- **Scale Adjustments:** Typography never drops below 16px anywhere in the application. Large sizes ensure text is comfortably readable from varied distances (e.g., tablet stands, wheelchair mounts, or classroom whiteboards).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows a calm, spacious fluid grid system engineered to eliminate visual claustrophobia and accidental edge taps.

- **Grid Architecture:** 
  - Desktop / Tablet Landscape: 8 to 12 columns with generous `2rem` to `2.5rem` outer canvas margins and `1.5rem` gutters.
  - Tablet Portrait / Large Handheld: 6 columns with `1.5rem` margins and `1.25rem` gutters.
  - Mobile Handheld: 4 columns with `1.25rem` outer margins and `1rem` gutters.
- **Breathing Space:** Elements are insulated by generous gaps (`space-lg` to `space-xl`) to clearly delineate conceptual sections. Related prompts, illustrations, and response buttons are grouped in cohesive spatial islands to avoid sensory overwhelm.
- **Target Boundaries:** Every interactive zone reserves at least `0.75rem` (`space-sm`) of blank buffer around its hit perimeter to prevent mis-clicks by users with fine-motor tremors or atypical coordination.

## Elevation & Depth

To maintain visual serenity, this design system avoids harsh dropshadows, complex 3D extrusions, or chaotic layered blurs that could agitate neurodivergent children.

- **Tonal Stepping:** Elevation is achieved through soft, warm tonal surfaces. The root background sits on `#FDFBF7`, primary card surfaces rise onto `#F7F4EB`, and active choice containers lift onto tinted pastels (`#E8F7F0`, `#FFF0E5`, `#F2EDFC`).
- **Tactile Padded Shadows:** Elevated cards and interactive buttons employ an ultra-soft, warm-tinted ambient drop shadow:
  - *Base Elevation:* `0 6px 16px rgba(118, 110, 101, 0.07)`
  - *Interactive Focus / Lift:* `0 10px 24px rgba(78, 186, 135, 0.15)`
- **Contour Outlines:** Delicate, low-contrast border strokes (`2px solid rgba(118, 110, 101, 0.10)`) give tactile definition to every interactive surface, ensuring spatial boundaries remain perceivable even for low-vision learners.

## Shapes

The design system embraces maximum roundedness (`roundedness: 3`), drawing inspiration from smooth river pebbles, rounded wooden toys, and plush stickers. 

- Sharp corners are entirely forbidden across all components.
- Buttons, chips, and quick-tap triggers default to continuous pill shapes (`rounded-full`).
- Content cards, emotion dials, dialogue callouts, and soothing interactive surfaces apply hyper-rounded aesthetics (`rounded-3xl` equivalent to 24px–32px corner radius) to eliminate all perceived visual sharpness, fostering a welcoming, child-safe sensory atmosphere.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Touch Sizing:** Buttons observe a strict minimum target height of `64px` and a minimum width of `80px` for mobile and tablet touch accessibility.
- **Primary Buttons:** High-contrast pill containers rendered in mint green (`#4EBA87`) with high-contrast warm white labels (`#FFFFFF`) and a subtle 3D bottom bevel (`border-b-4 border-[#3DA073]`) for an intuitive "pressable" tactile feel.
- **Secondary & Auxiliary Buttons:** Warm oatmeal backgrounds (`#F7F4EB`) with a `2px` border in `#766E65` (15% opacity), accompanied by an icon and text.

### Emotion Selection Chips & Cards
- **Emotion Cards:** Generously proportioned cards (`min-h-[140px]`) featuring the friendly cat mascot illustrating emotional states (e.g., Cat breathing peacefully, Cat needing a pause, Cat feeling fiery). 
- **Active State:** When selected, cards scale smoothly (`scale-102`) with a high-contrast `3px` solid stroke matching the emotion's spectrum color and an audible, gentle sensory sound effect (optional toggle).

### Input Fields & Sliders
- **Visual Thermometer / Emotion Dial:** Custom smooth slider with an oversized spherical thumb target (`56px` diameter) marked with the cat mascot's face changing expressions dynamically as the child drags the control across colors.
- **Text & Voice Inputs:** Clean ivory fields with `64px` height, large `20px` typography, and immediate voice-dictation mic shortcuts for children who find typing stressful.

### Mascots & Dialogue Bubbles
- **Guardian Cat Guide:** Positioned consistently in the lower right or anchored above tasks. Accompanied by speech bubbles with rounded corners (`rounded-3xl`) and distinct tail pointers, delivering simple, positive, one-sentence instructions.

### Feedback & Checkboxes
- **Toggles / Checkboxes:** Oversized rounded squares (`44px x 44px`, `rounded-2xl`) displaying an expressive paw-print stamp or green checkmark when activated, providing immediate, delightful confirmation.
